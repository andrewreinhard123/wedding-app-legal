Guest questions

How do I open a wedding?

Install The Big Day, or open the web link the couple sent. Enter the code they gave you. The current web app is andrew-melissa-wedding.expo.app. A couple’s own link, if they sent one, is the right link to use.

You can try the demo code EXAMPLE123. It opens a made-up wedding at Red Ridge in Smithville, Texas. It is not a real couple, and the people in it are not real guests.

Do I need an account?

No. You do not create an account, and you do not sign in.

Do I have to enter my name?

No. A display name is optional. If you leave it blank, your posts are not labeled with a name.

How do I share a photo or video?

Open the Photos area for that wedding. Add a photo or video from your camera or your library. You can add a caption. If you are offline, the upload can wait on your device until it can send.

How do I delete something I posted?

Open the menu on your post and delete it. That removes the photo or video, and its comments and likes, for people looking at that wedding. The app recognizes your posts with a random id stored on this device. If you delete the app’s data or switch phones, email us with the wedding code and a description of the post.

Who can see what I post?

People who have that wedding’s code or link, the couple, and platform admins. Other weddings do not share one combined gallery. The code works like a key. Anyone you forward it to can look.

What is Download All?

Couples and guests can download the visible photos and videos for that wedding. Save your own copy. We do not keep media indefinitely. Hidden Film Camera shots are not included until they are revealed. The details of deletion are on the refunds page and in the privacy policy.

What is the Film Camera?

If the couple uses it, those photos stay hidden until a reveal time they set. After the reveal, people with the code can see them. Until then, Download All skips them.

Where are the schedule, the story, and the seating?

In the app and on the site for the code you entered. You see that wedding, not every wedding. Some weddings also include travel notes or announcements. The couple chooses what to publish.

Does the app use my location?

No. Weather is for the venue. The app sends the venue’s coordinates to Open-Meteo. It does not read your phone’s GPS location. Notifications are optional. You can say no.

Are there ads? Do you sell my data?

We do not show ads. We do not sell personal data. We do not track you across other companies’ apps for advertising.

A child used the app, or a photo shows a child. What do I do?

The app is not directed to children under 13. Parents and guardians should supervise minors. If a child provided a name, a comment, or a photo, or if you need a specific photo of a child removed, email us with the wedding code and a description. We will delete it. More detail is in the privacy policy.

How do I get a Signature quote?

Email us with the date, the venue, and the features you want. Nothing is charged until you accept a quote and pay on the website. Quotes are valid for 30 days. A 50% deposit is required to begin work, paid via Stripe on the website. The quote states the features included; features not in the quote are a new quote, not an included round of changes.

What does storage cost?

Storage is a separate monthly subscription, required while guests can upload photos and videos. It is not required for a read-only published wedding. It renews monthly until you cancel. You can cancel at any time.

  • $9.99 per month includes 10 GB.
  • Each extra 10 GB block is $2 per month.

When you sign up, you choose what happens when storage is full. You can change the choice later.

  1. Delete the oldest photos and videos to make room. We warn you first and remind you to use Download All.
  2. Stop new uploads at the cap. This is the default.
  3. Auto-add 10 GB blocks at $2 per month each.

We warn you at 80% of your quota and again at 100%, whichever option you picked.

A wedding can be published without a storage subscription, but guests cannot upload photos or videos until a storage subscription is active. To remove an extra 10 GB block, go to admin or email support; the removal takes effect at the start of the next billing period if usage fits. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period, with no partial-month refund.

What happens if I cancel storage, or the wedding is called off?

If canceling or ending the wedding stops paid storage, you get at least 30 days’ warning at your billing email before we delete that wedding’s stored photos and videos. Use Download All during that time. We do not keep media indefinitely. Calling off the ceremony does not instantly delete the gallery if you are still paying for storage.

During the warning period, new uploads may continue unless the wedding is already full and set to stop new uploads. After the warning window ends, the non-media pages — the schedule, the story, the seating chart, and similar text — stay online in a read-only form. Only photos and videos follow the 30-day deletion rule.

How do I cancel or fix a charge?

Cancel storage from the billing page we send (Stripe’s customer portal), or email us from the billing email with your wedding code. If a charge looks wrong, email us before a card dispute if you can. Do not include the full card number. Charges appear on the card statement as THE BIG DAY. Listed prices exclude applicable sales tax; tax is added where required and shown before you pay.

Do guests pay anything?

No. Guests do not subscribe, and they do not pay a fee to post or to download.
