The Big Day

Wedding guest app · Draft date 15 November 2026 · Not yet in force

The Big Day is one app for many weddings. App Store display name: “The Big Day: Wedding Guest App.” A couple gets a code and a web link. Guests open the app or the link, enter the code, and see that wedding’s schedule, story, seating, and photo and video feed.

Guests do not create an account. Couples pay setup fees and optional storage on the website, not as an in-app purchase.

  • Privacy Policy — what guests and couples share, who sees photos, and how long media is kept. Use this address for App Store and Google Play privacy-policy fields after it is published and in force.
  • Terms of Service — the rules for using the app and the website, including acceptable use and liability.
  • Refund and Cancellation Policy — setup fees, the storage subscription, and what happens to photos and videos when paid storage stops.
  • Support — how to contact us, guest questions, and couple questions.
