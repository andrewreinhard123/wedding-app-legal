Common questions

  • How do I share a photo? Open the Photos tab and tap Add photos. You can take a new photo or video with the camera, or choose from your library.
  • How do I delete something I posted? Tap the ⋮ menu at the top-right of your photo in the feed and choose Delete photo.
  • Where are the schedule, hotels, and shuttle details? See the Schedule and Travel tabs.
  • What is the Film Camera? Photos taken with it stay hidden until they're "developed" and revealed to everyone at the set time after the wedding.
  • Do I need an account? No — there's no sign-in. Adding your name is optional.
