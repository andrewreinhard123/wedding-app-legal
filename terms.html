1. The service

The Big Day lets many couples each run a wedding inside one product. A couple receives a code and a web link. Guests open the app or the link, enter the code, and see that wedding. What they see can include the schedule, the story, seating, and a photo and video feed. Some weddings also include travel notes, announcements, weather at the venue, reminders, or a Film Camera reveal.

Guests do not create an account and do not sign in. A display name is optional. A random guest id stays on the device so that device can manage and delete its own posts.

We may add, change, or retire a feature. Wedding-day reliability is a design goal. It is not a guarantee that the app, the website, uploads, notifications, or Download All will work every minute of an event.

5. Storage

Storage is a separate monthly subscription, required while guests can upload photos and videos. It is not required for a read-only published wedding. It renews each month until the couple cancels. The couple may cancel at any time. There is no separate cancellation fee.

  • $9.99 per month includes 10 GB.
  • Each extra 10 GB block is $2 per month.

At sign-up the couple chooses what happens when storage is full. They can change this later.

  1. Delete oldest photos and videos to make room. We warn first and remind the couple to use Download All. Then the oldest media may be deleted.
  2. Stop new uploads at the cap. This is the default. Existing media stays. New uploads wait until there is room or the couple changes the plan.
  3. Auto-add 10 GB blocks at $2 per month each, so uploads can continue. The added blocks stay on the monthly bill until they are removed.

For every option, we warn the couple at 80% of the quota and again at 100%.

To remove an extra 10 GB block, go to admin or email support. The removal takes effect at the start of the next billing period, and only if your current usage fits in the remaining quota. We will not remove a block mid-period if your files still need that space. Delete or download files first so usage fits, then we can remove the block.

Storage cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period. Future monthly charges stop once the cancellation takes effect. There is no partial-month refund and no separate cancellation fee. Cancel at any time from the Stripe customer portal or by emailing us from your billing email with your wedding code.

If we raise the list price, existing subscribers keep their current price for at least 12 months after the increase is announced. We give at least 30 days' notice of a price increase by email to the billing address. After that 12-month period, the new price may apply. Where a law requires a longer notice period or a right to cancel, we follow that law.

A wedding can be published without a storage subscription. Without active paid storage, the wedding stays viewable in a read-only form: guests cannot upload photos or videos. A storage subscription is required while guests can upload. Setup does not include ongoing media hosting.

6. Media, Download All, and deletion

Download All is a core feature for couples and for guests. It covers visible photos and videos. Unrevealed Film Camera photos stay out of Download All until they are revealed. Save a copy you control. We do not keep wedding media indefinitely.

If a couple cancels storage, or a wedding is cancelled or ended in a way that stops paid storage, we give at least 30 days’ warning before we delete that wedding’s stored photos and videos. We send the warning to the billing email. Download All remains available during that warning. After the warning, we delete those files.

During that warning period, new uploads may continue unless the wedding is already at 100% of its quota and the couple's storage-full choice is set to stop new uploads. Download All remains available during the entire warning window.

After paid storage stops and the media warning window ends, the non-media pages — the schedule, the story, the seating chart, and similar text — stay online in a read-only form. They are not deleted on the same schedule as the photos and videos.

Choosing “delete oldest” is an instruction from the couple to remove guest media when space runs out, after the warning described above. The couple should tell guests if older posts may be removed. That path is not the 30-day end-of-subscription window.

A guest’s own deletion, or a moderation removal, takes effect when we process it. It does not wait 30 days.
