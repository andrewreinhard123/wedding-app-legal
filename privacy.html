The Big Day is one app and website for many weddings. Each couple has a code and a link. Guests who enter that code can see that wedding’s schedule, story, seating, and photo and video feed. This policy explains what information the product handles. The Terms of Service and the Refund and Cancellation Policy explain the rules and the fees.

1. The short version

  • Guests do not create an account and do not sign in.
  • A display name is optional.
  • A random id stored on the device lets a guest manage and delete their own posts.
  • We do not sell personal data. We do not show ads. We do not use data for advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • Couples pay on the website through Stripe. We do not receive full card numbers.
  • Photo and video files are stored on Cloudflare R2. Wedding details and gallery rows are stored on Supabase.
  • Download All is a core feature. We do not keep wedding media indefinitely. See section 8.
  • The app is not directed to children under 13.

2. Who this policy covers

Guests use a wedding code or link. They can look at the wedding and, if they choose, post photos, videos, and short reactions. They do not pay us.

Couples (and anyone they authorize to set up the wedding) buy setup and, if they want it, monthly storage. They give us names, event details, a billing email, and payment through Stripe.

People listed on a seating chart or schedule did not necessarily open the app. The couple chose what to publish about them. Anyone with the code can see those pages.

3. Information that stays on the device

These items stay on the phone, tablet, or browser until you send them or clear the app’s data:

  • The display name you optionally type, so you do not have to retype it.
  • Day-of reminders you turn on. Those reminders are scheduled locally.
  • Photos and videos waiting to upload while you are offline. They leave the device when the upload goes through, or when you cancel them.
  • The random guest id, until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall in a way that removes local data.

You can change or clear the display name in the app. Clearing app data creates a new guest id. It does not erase posts already uploaded. Email us if you need those removed and the device no longer has the old id.

6. Who can see a wedding

People who have that wedding’s code or link can see the pages and the visible photo and video feed. The couple can see and manage their wedding. Platform admins can see content as needed to operate and moderate the service. A different wedding’s guests do not get a combined feed of every wedding.

Treat the code and the link like a key. Anyone who has the key can look. Guests often forward an invitation. We cannot promise that a guest will keep the code private.

Photo and video files are stored at unguessable addresses and are served from Cloudflare R2. A person who does not have the code should not be able to browse their way to a wedding. A person who receives a file address, or who uses Download All while they have access, can save a copy. After someone has a copy, we cannot pull it back off their device.

Some weddings use a Film Camera. Those photos stay hidden until the reveal time set for that wedding. Until then, other guests do not see them, and Download All does not include them. After the reveal, they are visible like other posts. The couple and platform admins can still moderate them.

The couple, and platform admins, may hide or remove any post. A guest can delete their own posts from the device that has the matching guest id.

7. Weather

Venue weather comes from Open-Meteo. The app requests a forecast for the wedding venue’s coordinates. It does not read your device’s GPS location, and it does not send your name or guest id to Open-Meteo.

The request is made from your device. Open-Meteo therefore receives the venue coordinates and the usual network information that comes with a web request, including an IP address. We use this only to show venue weather. We do not use it for advertising or tracking.

8. How long we keep information

Download All (photos and videos) is a core feature for couples and for guests. Save your own copy. We do not keep wedding photos and videos indefinitely.

While paid storage is active, we keep that wedding’s uploaded photos and videos so guests can view them and download them.

If the couple chose “delete oldest” when storage is full, we warn them first and remind them to use Download All. We may then delete the oldest photos and videos to make room. That can happen during an active subscription. It is separate from the 30-day wind-down below. The couple can change this choice later. The default choice is to stop new uploads at the cap, not to delete old files. See the Refund and Cancellation Policy for the three choices and the 80% and 100% warnings.

If a couple cancels storage, or a wedding is cancelled or ended in a way that stops paid storage, we give the couple at least 30 days’ warning before we delete that wedding’s stored photos and videos. We send the warning to the billing email. Download All stays available during that warning so the couple and guests can save the media. After the warning period, we delete those stored photos and videos.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age in the user’s region if that age is higher. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Parents and guardians should supervise any use by minors at a wedding. A wedding app is often opened by adults around children. An adult may upload a photo that includes a child. That photo is the uploader’s content. A parent or guardian can ask us to remove a specific photo of their child.

If a child under 13 provided data — for example a display name, a comment, or a photo — email andrewreinhard0@gmail.com. Include the wedding code, the child’s display name if any, and which posts to delete. We will delete that data. We may ask for enough detail to find the right wedding and the right posts, because there is no child account to look up.

11. Service providers

These providers process data for us so the product can run. They are not allowed to use it for their own advertising. We name the providers we actually plan to use.

  • Stripe — payment processing for couples’ setup fees and storage subscriptions. Couples pay on the website, not inside the iOS or Android app. We do not receive full card numbers.
  • Cloudflare R2 — storage of photo and video files. Views and downloads are served from R2.
  • Supabase — wedding metadata and gallery row metadata (for example captions, display names, guest ids, comments, likes, moderation flags, and push tokens). Supabase is not the long-term store for the media files.
  • Apple and Google — distribution of the iOS and Android apps and, if a guest turns notifications on, delivery of pushes. They handle store payments if any. Our setup fees and storage subscriptions are intended to be purchased on the web, not as in-app purchases. The stores’ own privacy policies cover a store account.
  • Expo — if a guest turns notifications on, the app registers an Expo push token. Expo relays that notification to Apple or Google. We use this only for wedding alerts the person opted into. If push shipping changes, this sentence must be updated.
  • Open-Meteo — venue weather only, using the wedding venue’s coordinates, never the guest’s device location. See section 7 for the IP address that a normal web request reveals.

12. No sale, no ads, no cross-app tracking

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not show advertising in the app. We do not track guests or couples across other companies’ apps or websites for ads.

We do share information in the ways this policy describes: with guests of the same wedding, with the couple who hosts it, with the providers in section 11, with someone you ask us to contact, and when the law requires it. We may also share information to investigate abuse, enforce our terms, or protect guests, couples, and the service.

Where a U.S. state privacy law uses the words “sale” or “share,” we do not sell or share personal data as those laws define those words. To ask a question or to submit a request, email andrewreinhard0@gmail.com.

14. Your choices and rights

Guests can skip the display name, decline notifications, choose not to upload, and delete their own posts on the device that posted them. Couples can cancel storage as described in the Refund and Cancellation Policy, change what happens when storage is full, and email us about their wedding pages.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal data about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You may have the right to appeal our decision. You may have the right to complain to a data protection authority or other regulator. You will not be discriminated against for making a request, where the law forbids that. Some laws let you use an authorized agent. We may ask the agent for proof.

Email andrewreinhard0@gmail.com to use these rights. Include the wedding code and enough detail for us to find the right information. Guests do not have accounts, so we may not be able to find a post from a name alone. We may need to confirm that you are the person the request is about, or the parent or guardian of a child.

We will respond within the time the applicable law requires. This draft does not invent a shorter internal deadline.
