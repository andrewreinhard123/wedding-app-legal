This page is about money and about what happens to photos and videos when paid storage stops. Guests do not pay. Couples pay on the website through Stripe. Setup fees and storage subscriptions are not sold as in-app purchases on the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store full card numbers. Stripe processes the card.

Prices below are in U.S. dollars. The amount a couple authorizes is the amount shown on the website checkout before they pay. If this page and the checkout total ever disagree, the checkout total is the amount authorized for that purchase, and we will correct this page.

Listed prices exclude applicable sales tax. Tax is added where required, and the amount is shown before you pay.

Charges appear on the card statement as THE BIG DAY.

1. Setup fees

Setup is a one-time fee to create the couple’s wedding in The Big Day: their pages, their code, and their link. It is separate from monthly storage.

TierPriceIncluded changes
Classic $50 3 rounds of changes, then $10 per additional change
Custom about $125. The exact price is shown before you pay. 3 rounds of changes, then $10 per additional change
Signature $250 and up. We quote each feature before we charge. 3 rounds of changes, then $10 per additional change

What a “round of changes” means

Each tier includes three rounds of changes after the wedding is set up. A further change is $10.

One round of changes is one consolidated list of edits submitted together by the couple, or by a person the couple has authorized in writing or by email. A later, separate list starts a new round. Requests must come from the couple using the billing or support email we have on file, or from a person they have authorized.

After the three included rounds, each additional consolidated list is $10. The fee is billed on the website via a Stripe invoice or payment link, and is due before the change is made. It is not an in-app purchase.

Signature quotes

Signature starts at $250 and goes up based on the features the couple wants. We send a quote first. Paying a Signature invoice on the website is acceptance of that quote.

Quotes are valid for 30 days from the date we send them. A 50% deposit is required to begin work, paid on the website via Stripe; this deposit is credited toward the final quoted price. The quote states the features included. Later edits use the three included rounds, then the $10 fee, like the other tiers. Features not in the accepted quote are a new quote, not an included round.

Refunds of setup fees

Any statutory or cooling-off right a consumer already has is preserved. These terms do not waive rights a law gives you.

Setup fees are refunded in stages, based on how far along setup is at the time you cancel:

  • Before setup work starts: the full setup fee is refunded.
  • After setup work has started, but before the wedding is published: 50% of the setup fee is refunded.
  • After the wedding is published: the fee is not refunded, except where a law requires otherwise.

These stages apply regardless of the reason: a cancellation, a decision to call off the real-world wedding, or dissatisfaction with the design. Used or unused change rounds do not change the refund percentage.

For Signature, the same stages apply to the quoted setup fee. The 50% deposit is credited toward that fee. If a 50% refund is due, the kept half may be the deposit.

2. Storage subscription

Storage is a separate monthly subscription, required while guests can upload photos and videos. It is not required for a read-only published wedding. The couple can cancel at any time. We do not charge a separate cancellation fee.

ItemPriceWhat it includes
Storage $9.99 per month 10 GB for that wedding’s photos and videos
Extra space $2 per month for each extra block 10 GB per block, added to the same monthly subscription

A wedding can be published without a storage subscription. Without active paid storage, the wedding stays viewable in a read-only form: guests cannot upload photos or videos. A storage subscription is required while guests can upload. Setup does not include ongoing media hosting.

If we raise the list price, existing subscribers keep their current price for at least 12 months after the increase is announced. We give at least 30 days’ notice by email to the billing address. After that 12-month period, the new price may apply. Where a law requires a longer notice period or a right to cancel, we follow that law.

When storage is full

At sign-up the couple chooses one of these options. They can change it later. The default is option 2.

  1. Delete oldest photos and videos to make room. We warn the couple first and remind them to use Download All. After that warning, the oldest photos and videos may be deleted so new ones fit. This can happen while the subscription is still active. Guests should know that older posts can disappear under this option.
  2. Stop new uploads at the cap. This is the default. When the wedding reaches its quota, new uploads stop. Photos and videos already stored stay in place. Uploads can resume if someone deletes media, if the couple changes this setting, or if they add space.
  3. Auto-add 10 GB blocks at $2 per month each. When an upload would pass the cap, another block is added and the monthly price goes up by $2 for each block. Uploads can continue.

We warn the couple at 80% of the quota and again at 100%, for all three options. The warning is so they can download, delete, or buy space before a cap is hit.

To remove an extra 10 GB block, go to admin or email support. The removal takes effect at the start of the next billing period, and only if your current usage fits in the remaining quota. We will not remove a block mid-period if your files still need that space. Delete or download files first so usage fits, then we can remove the block.

How to cancel storage

The couple may cancel at any time. Cancel on the billing page we provide (a Stripe customer portal) or by emailing thebigday.support@gmail.com from the billing email, with the wedding code.

Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period. Future monthly charges stop once the cancellation takes effect. There is no partial-month refund.

Canceling storage does not, by itself, refund the one-time setup fee. Setup refunds follow the stage-based rule in section 1.

3. What happens to photos and videos

Download All is a core feature for couples and for guests. It downloads the wedding’s visible photos and videos. Film Camera shots that are still hidden are not included until they are revealed. Use Download All to keep your own copy.

We do not keep wedding media indefinitely.

If paid storage stops

If a couple cancels storage, or a wedding is cancelled or ended in a way that stops paid storage, we give at least 30 days’ warning before we delete that wedding’s stored photos and videos. We send the warning to the billing email. During those days, Download All remains available so the couple and guests can save the media. When the warning period ends, we delete the stored photos and videos.

Keep the billing inbox working, and check spam, during the month you cancel. A downloaded copy on your own devices is the copy that remains after we delete ours.

During that warning period, new uploads may continue unless the wedding is already at 100% of its quota and the couple's storage-full choice is set to stop new uploads. Download All remains available during the entire warning window.

After paid storage stops and the media warning window ends, the non-media pages — the schedule, the story, the seating chart, and similar text — stay online in a read-only form. They are not deleted on the same schedule as the photos and videos.

Calling off a real-world wedding does not, by itself, delete media on the day you decide to call it off. Deletion of media follows the rule above when paid storage stops. If the couple keeps paying for storage, this 30-day deletion clock does not start merely because the ceremony will not happen.

Other ways media is removed

  • The couple chose “delete oldest.” After a warning and a reminder to use Download All, the oldest photos and videos may be deleted to free space. That does not wait for the 30-day end-of-subscription window.
  • A guest deletes their own post. We remove that post and its stored file. Other people’s posts stay.
  • The couple or a platform admin hides or removes a post, including prohibited content. We remove it from the wedding. That does not start a 30-day public archive of the removed post.

4. Billing mistakes

If a charge looks wrong, email thebigday.support@gmail.com before opening a card dispute if you can. Include the wedding code, the billing email, the amount, and the date. Do not send the full card number. We would rather correct a mistaken charge than dispute one blindly.

This policy does not set a special goodwill refund beyond the rules above. A right that a card network or a consumer law already gives you still applies.
